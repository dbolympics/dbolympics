{{SEC_METHOD}}Methodology

The data
Tidewick Commerce in 251 tables, at scale factors 1, 10, 100 and 1000: from 6.1 million to 4.1 billion rows.
The run
A fresh system loads everything through 2024, then 2025 one quarter at a time. After every load it answers all 409 queries {{REPS}}, and it is never restarted. The figures show the run after the last load.
Correctness
After the last load, every result is compared by value with a reference result. Integers must match exactly; other numbers may differ by at most 0.0001 + 0.0001 × |expected|. A wrong result counts as a failed query.
Failures
A query still running after its timeout ({{TIMEOUT}}) is aborted. In every measure of speed, a timed-out query counts as taking the full timeout, and a failed or wrong one as taking twice the slowest correct time of that query.
Figure 1
Queries per second is 409 over the time all queries take together after the last load. The cost is that time at the machine's price per hour (methodology, Machines).
